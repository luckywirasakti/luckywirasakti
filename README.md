## 🚀 Engineering Highlights

- **Scaled backends to millions of requests/day** — built and operated the backend for a nationwide FMCG field-operations platform, with nightly cron pipelines reconciling tens of thousands of records against internal enterprise systems.
- **Distributed 50,000 vouchers with zero double-redemption** — a campaign delivery system over the official WhatsApp Business API, using pessimistic locking and non-auto-commit transactions to stay correct under concurrent load.
- **Fraud-resistant payment disbursement** — a Midtrans Iris payout service enforcing one-claim-per-verified-phone with row locking and request throttling.
- **LLMs & RAG in production** — an automated content generator and a RAG chatbot with persistent vector memory, integrated into real content workflows.
- **Integration middleware** — an OAuth2 webhook-routing layer sustaining tens of thousands of calls with automatic retry for zero data loss.
- **Shipped the delivery pipeline** — Dokploy staging previews and self-hosted GitLab runners wired into CI/CD for hands-off deployments.
